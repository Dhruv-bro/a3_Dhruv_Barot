## Strategy notes

1. **AWS is your #1 target.** "Cloud Support Associate" is *their* job title; they hire cohorts year-round in Toronto and Vancouver — a Canadian citizen/PR can apply without visa friction, and the role has a defined promotion path to Cloud Support Engineer.
2. **Google Waterloo** is the most underrated Canadian target — a large share of Google Cloud's global support engineering sits there.
3. **Apply within 72 hours of posting.** Support roles get hundreds of applicants; recency matters more than perfection.
4. **Banks and telecoms** (RBC, TD, Telus, Bell) run big Azure/AWS estates and get far fewer new-grad applicants than FAANG — better odds, real cloud work.
5. Track everything in the CSV the job agent produces — company, date applied, contact, status, follow-up date. Follow up once at day 7.
