My love,

I’ve started this letter more times than I can count, because “sorry” never feels big enough on its own. But it’s the truest place to start, so I’ll just say it. I’m sorry.

I’m sorry I hurt you, and I’m sorry for every moment I made you feel like you had to carry it alone. You deserve so much better than that, and you deserve it from me.

You don’t have to forgive me right away. I’m not writing this to rush you. I just need you to hear it clearly, with no excuses: I know I got it wrong, and I’m going to do better.

I keep coming back to these pictures. Two pumpkin spice drinks, that low golden light, and the two of us not doing anything special at all. It was such an ordinary afternoon, and it’s one of my favourite memories. That’s what you do. You turn ordinary days into the ones I hold on to.

I never want to be the reason that light goes out. So here’s what I’m promising you, and I mean every word:

  • I’ll listen first and explain later.
  • I’ll show up when I say I will.
  • I’ll never let you fall asleep wondering if we’re okay.
  • I’ll keep planning more afternoons like these.

Take all the time you need. I’ll be right here, holding your coffee order, hoping you’ll let me hold your hand again too.

So… will you forgive me?
